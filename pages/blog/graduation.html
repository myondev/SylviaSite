It's been a bit since I last wrote a blog post, but I think I'm back in the groove, hopefully.
Anyways, I have graduated high school!!
Now I can actually be an adult, and do adult things like taxes and getting jobs and mortgages, which sound very fun.
I don't know anything about being an adult, nobody ever taught me!

I'll talk about the actual event here. How did graduation go?
Terribly!! But it worked out in the end!
So, a few days before graduation, I was supposed to grab my cap and gown from the school. I don't know why they simply weren't giving it on the same day.
The next day we were supposed to rehearse for the graduation, which was apparently MANDATORY otherwise you couldn't even walk. After that was the actual graduation day.
What happened? I showed up for neither of the days, because I simply didn't care enough to.
Entirely because I live 2 entire hours away from my school, and my parents both have work and I would've needed to have to wait until they got off work to go home.
I just wanted to have a break from school!
Why did I even need to rehearse for the graduation?? All you literally do is sit on a chair for an entire 2 hours, walk on stage, shake hands, grab your diploma and leave. I don't understand what's so mandatory about that.
As for the actual graduation day. I arrived about 2 hours early, so I wasn't late. Graduation starts pretty late, at 5 PM.
It was like 3 PM and I came to school fully dressed in an awesome as heck suit and tie, watching all the other non-senior students leave school for the last time. I love suits, they look so awesome. I felt so cool wearing mine...
Anyways I came there, expecting not to be able to walk on stage, and disappointing my family with my inability to do so. People started arriving wearing their caps and gowns.
People were asking where my graduation stuff was, I just told them I wasn't allowed to walk for some reason.
Oh and everyone had some really cool designs they put ontop of their cap. Wish I could do the same, but again, I'm lazy!
Eventually, it became time to get checked in. The staff asked me "Where's your cap and gown?" I just told the staff that I wasn't able to come on that day.
Luckily I was still able to receive them, and still even walk despite everything. Getting that stuff was a journey though.
I had to run down to my counselors office and ask for my counselor to get me the graduation stuff, where I was told to go to a different person, and ask for it, run back up to the check in area who told me to look for an entirely different person, then I had to run to the entire opposite end of the school just to finally get my cap and gown.
And I was let in, despite literally not attending the "mandatory" (and useless) rehearsal. Well, that's it then. We went down to the school's stadium, and sat through these random life lesson speeches from people in my school I have never met. I guess the speeches were nice.
Then I simply just waited for my row to be called up, they say my name, I walk up the stage, grab my fancy diploma-less folder, and then just sat back down and waited for every other senior to be called up, then we threw our caps into the air, yay!

And that was literally it. I had to get my actual diploma individually, which you had to get yourself rather than on stage. I talked a bit to my friends, and then I left. Straight to back home.
Was it fun? Well I got to wear a suit and that's all I care about. Oh and no more high school, yay!
I think I'll just get back to drawing and game deving. Though this time I won't have to sleep at 2 am nor wake up at 5 am and get home at 8 pm, which is good, I hated that!
